**Description:** Reproducibility package supporting the study “Beyond Accuracy in IoT Intrusion Detection: Property-Based Assurance of Predictive and SHAP Explanation Robustness”. The package contains the frozen PF4-A1 scientific source, read-only evidence-to-table analysis, derived results, figures, protocol metadata, integrity manifests, and instructions for reproducing manuscript-grade PF4 results from the sealed evidence archive. The executed assurance scope is PF0-PF4. PF5 was not executed. Random Forest was not executed in PF4. Third-party CICIoT2023 and TON_IoT datasets are not redistributed.
